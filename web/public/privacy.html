LoopedIn Privacy & Contact

Your privacy.

Last updated: June 2026

Draft for review. This policy is a starting point that reflects how LoopedIn is designed to handle data. Have it reviewed by legal counsel before public launch and before any payments or cashout go live.

LoopedIn is a paid community-research network. People answer short questions about their lives and communities and earn points; organizations ask questions and receive anonymized insights. Protecting the people who answer is the core of how the product is built.

The promise

Your identity is always anonymous to organizations. Organizations never see your name and never see your full address. They only ever see aggregate results, profile-attribute summaries, and a coded response ID (for example R-2841).

What we collect

  • Account: email and phone number (used to save your wallet and contact you about your activity).
  • Profile (optional): birth year (used only to confirm you are 18+), income bracket, location, household, languages, education, public-benefit usage, and lived-experience tags. These power question matching.
  • Your answers: the text or voice responses you choose to submit.
  • Points activity: what you have earned.

How we use it

  • To match you with relevant questions and credit your points.
  • To produce anonymized, aggregate insight for organizations.
  • To contact you about your activity. We do not sell your personal information.

What organizations can and cannot see

  • Never: your name or full address.
  • Location: only city-level. Your ZIP is used to match questions but is not shown to organizations.
  • Race / ethnicity: collected only to report aggregate breakdowns — it is never used as a targeting filter.
  • Responses: shown with a coded ID and attribute chips only, or rolled up into summaries.

Anonymization & deletion

Responses are de-identified when they are synthesized into reports, and the link to your identity is severed at that point. You can delete your profile at any time; because already-published reports never contained your identity, they are not affected.

Points & payments

Points are shown throughout the product (100 points = $1.00). Cashout is coming soon — points accrue today, but no money leaves the platform yet. When cashout activates, additional terms and identity/tax requirements will apply.

Children

LoopedIn is for adults. You must be 18 or older; a birth-year age gate enforces this.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS with standard security protections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we design to minimize what is collected and what is exposed.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product grows. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
